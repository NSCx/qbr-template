Priority Area What the Data Says Execution Focus (Next 90 Days) Target KPI
Recover Tier-1 Decliners Biggest YoY value erosion from INTEL (−$1.32M), HP (−$1.16M), CRSAR, and other major brands. Joint QBRs with vendor + sales leaders, account-level win/loss review, weekly deal-recovery pipeline, promo and pricing interventions. Recover 25–35% of lost revenue in top 4 decliners by next quarter.
Stabilize Margin-Leaking / Negative Lines COOLM moved to negative sales (−$2.9K), with several brands at near-zero run-rates or fully inactive. SKU rationalization, return/credit root-cause checks, floor-price governance, discontinue non-performing listings. Eliminate negative net sales; restore all active lines to positive monthly run-rate.
Reduce Concentration Risk Growth is led by a small cluster (ASUS, LENOV, PNY, KINGS, GIGA), while many brands shrink. Diversification plan across mid-tier brands (LG, WD, SAPHR, TEAM, SEAGA) with bundled offers and channel-specific plays. Increase non-top-5 share of growth by +10 pts.
Re-energize Developing Brand Portfolio Several developing brands recorded softer YoY outcomes this half (e.g., BRTHR, NETGR, HTC, APPLE). Segment portfolio into defend / incubate / accelerate; assign owner per brand; apply minimum quarterly revenue thresholds. Improve aggregate YoY across developing brands by +15 pts.
Executive focus: protect gains from fast-growing brands while executing a structured recovery plan on high-value decliners.
