Slide 5

Operational performance

Show delivery quality, efficiency, and execution reliability.

Suggested metrics

  • • SLA or turnaround time
  • • Productivity / throughput
  • • Quality or defect rate
  • • Capacity / utilisation
  • • Major delivery milestones

Commentary formula

State the result, explain the driver, then give the implication. Example: “Turnaround improved by 9 percent due to workflow automation, which reduced backlog risk heading into next quarter.”
